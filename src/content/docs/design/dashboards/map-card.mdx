## Usage

The map card answers one question: where is everyone? It puts a marker on the map for every entity with a location, like a person, a device tracker, or a zone. Selecting a marker opens the more-info dialog of the entity. People can pan and zoom the map, and the buttons on the side reset the view and turn clustering on or off.

Use it when the location itself matters. If people only want to know whether someone is home, a [tile card](/design/dashboards/tile-card/) or a [glance card](/design/dashboards/glance-card/) of the person entity says that in far less room. A map takes a lot of space for one piece of information, so give it a reason to be there.

The map follows the theme of Home Assistant by default. In a light theme the map is light, in a dark theme it is dark.

### Markers

A person or a device tracker shows as a round marker with the initials of its name, or its picture when it has one. A zone shows as its icon. With `label_mode` on an entity, the marker shows the state, an attribute, or the icon instead of the name.

Markers that are close together are combined into one bubble with a count, until you zoom in. Turn this off with `cluster: false` when you have only a few entities.

### Path of previous locations

With `hours_to_show`, the map draws a line through where each entity has been in that many hours. It is useful for something that moves, like a car or a lawn mower. Keep the number of hours low: a week of locations turns into a tangle that tells nobody anything.

### Theme mode

With `theme_mode`, the map is always `light` or always `dark`, whatever the theme. The default, `auto`, follows the theme. A dark map can work well on a wall tablet at night, but forcing a mode means it no longer matches the rest of the dashboard.

### Map style

The map has a few built-in looks, picked with `map_style`: `default`, `colorful`, `natural`, `muted`, `gray`, and `toner`. Each has a light and a dark version, and the theme mode picks which one shows. The default style uses colors that fit the Home Assistant theme. In YAML, `map_style` can also be a mapping that starts from one of these styles and changes its colors.

### Content

- Add the entities that need to be on the map, and nothing more. Every extra marker makes the one people look for harder to find.
- Add zones when they give the markers meaning, like the home and the office. A marker next to a zone reads at a glance.
- Use `name` on an entity when its initials are ambiguous, like two people whose names start with the same letter.

## Configuration

- `entities` (list): the entities to show. Each is an entity ID, or a mapping with `entity` and the options below. Required, unless you use `show_all` or `geo_location_sources`.
- `geo_location_sources` (list): the geolocation sources to show all entities of, like earthquakes or wildfires from a feed. Use `all` for every source.
- `show_all` (boolean): shows every entity with a location. Can't be combined with `entities` or `geo_location_sources`. Defaults to `false`.
- `title` (string): the title above the map.
- `aspect_ratio` (string): the height of the map compared to its width, like `16:9` or `50%`.
- `default_zoom` (number): the zoom level when the map opens. Defaults to `14`.
- `theme_mode` (string): `auto`, `light`, or `dark`. Defaults to `auto`.
- `map_style` (string or mapping): the look of the map. Defaults to `default`.
- `hours_to_show` (number): how many hours of previous locations to draw. Defaults to `0`, no path.
- `auto_fit` (boolean): moves and zooms the map whenever an entity moves, so all of them stay in view. Defaults to `false`.
- `fit_zones` (boolean): keeps the zones in view as well when the map fits the entities. Defaults to `false`.
- `show_zone_radius` (boolean): always draws the circle that shows the size of a zone. Defaults to `false`.
- `cluster` (boolean): combines markers that are close together. Defaults to `true`.
- `scale_ruler` (boolean): shows a ruler with the scale of the map. Defaults to `false`.
- `conditions` (list): shows the entities only when all conditions are met.

The options of an entity in `entities`:

- `entity` (string, required): the entity to show.
- `name` (string): the label on the marker.
- `color` (string): the color of the marker and its path.
- `label_mode` (string): what the marker shows: `name`, `state`, `attribute`, or `icon`. Defaults to `name`.
- `attribute` (string): the attribute to show, with `label_mode: attribute`.
- `unit` (string): the unit to show after the attribute.
- `focus` (boolean): keeps this entity in view when the map fits. Defaults to `true`.

```yaml
type: map
hours_to_show: 24
entities:
  - person.alex
  - entity: person.sam
    color: "#93c47d"
  - zone.home
  - zone.work
```

## In code

The card lives in `src/panels/lovelace/cards/hui-map-card.ts`, and the element is `<hui-map-card>`. The map itself is `<ha-map>` in `src/components/map/`, which is also used by the more-info dialog of a person, and by the location editors in the settings. It draws a vector map with MapLibre, and falls back to Leaflet on devices that can't show a vector map. The styles live in `src/common/map/map-styles.ts`.

In the sections view, the map takes the full width and 4 rows. It can shrink to 6 columns and 2 rows.
